## How the guarantee works

One primitive, however it is presented to the customer.

#### Issued as a Pass

Tokenised at creation with a unique code, traceable through its whole life. One standard, seven ways to present it.

Chips: Coupon · Voucher · Deal · Ticket · Reward · Card · Token

#### Held in a Vault

Every Pass a customer owns sits in one place, ready to present — alongside the balance they spend on verified offers.

#### Consumed once

Validated at the counter and immediately spent. A copy will not validate, and a Pass that was never issued does not exist.
