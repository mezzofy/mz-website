## How the partnership works

#### Connect once

REST or MCP, against a sandbox you get on signup. Certification before you go live in production.

#### Your merchants inherit the capability

One agreement covers the merchants on your platform. They do not each sign separately, and they do not each integrate.

#### You earn on every Pass sold

Revenue share settled at the point of sale rather than at redemption, so your earnings do not depend on whether a customer turns up at the counter.

> **Support stays where the relationship is.** You keep the merchant relationship and the commercial terms with them. Mezzofy handles Pass validation, consumer support and settlement.

## Questions partners ask

**Q: Who owns the merchant relationship?**  
A: You do. Your commercial terms with your merchants are yours. Mezzofy handles Pass validation, consumer support and settlement, and never contacts your merchants directly without your say-so.

**Q: Does each merchant sign with Mezzofy?**  
A: No. One partnership agreement covers the merchants on your platform. They opt in through your interface and inherit the terms.

**Q: How long is certification?**  
A: Typically two to three weeks from a working sandbox integration. We test issuance, redemption, failure handling and settlement reconciliation before production keys are issued.

**Q: Is the partnership exclusive?**  
A: Not by default. Exclusivity in a market or category is available against volume commitments and is priced accordingly.

**Q: What if a merchant on my platform refuses a valid Pass?**  
A: The customer is credited automatically, and the merchant is flagged. Repeated refusals suspend their listing. We will tell you before it reaches that point.
