## Create, send, redeem, reconcile

#### Create

Any Pass type, your artwork, your terms. Templates for the common cases, full control when you need it. Bulk import for lists you already hold.

#### Send

Email, SMS and WhatsApp built in. QR export for print. API for your own app. Messaging costs pass through at cost.

#### Redeem

The same Operator App and POS integration every Pass uses. Registered outlets only. Every validation is live and single-use.

#### Reconcile

Issued, sent, redeemed, expired — per Pass, per campaign, per outlet. Exportable, and billed against the same numbers.

## When CMS is the right choice, and when it is not

It comes down to which customers you are trying to reach. The ones you already have go one way. The ones you do not have yet go the other.

**Infographic — Two directions — customers you have (CMS) vs customers you do not (exchange)**

_Route: Customers you already have → CMS_

### CMS

_Label: Private · metered_
- **Your list** members, staff, past customers
- **You create the Pass** your artwork, your terms
- **You send it**
Chips: Email · SMS · WhatsApp · QR · Your app

- You want to reward a list privately
- Member or staff programmes should not be public
- You need full control of artwork, copy and timing
- You need Passes inside your own app

_Centre: **Your business** one account, both routes_

_Route: Customers you do not have yet → The exchange_

### The exchange

_Label: Free to list_
- **Set your limits** or your voucher value
- **AI Coupon creates and places** or we list what you made
- **Buyers find it**
Chips: Marketplace · Enterprise apps · Tap points · AI assistants

- You want new customers, not existing ones
- You would rather not build campaigns — Zero Fee is automatic
- You want to sell vouchers to enterprise buyers
- You are near a tap point and want walk-ins

> **Most merchants run both.** CMS for the list they have, the exchange for the customers they do not. The two inventories never mix, so a private member offer cannot leak onto the marketplace.
