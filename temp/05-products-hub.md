# Everything the exchange runs on.

Each product does one job. Together they take an offer from creation to the moment it is consumed at a counter, with the same guarantee the whole way.

_Label: The unit_

### Pass

The tokenised, single-use primitive every offer is issued as. One standard, seven presentations.

→ links to coupon-serial.html (→ pass.html)

_Label: For customers_

### Vault

Where a customer keeps every Pass they own, and the prepaid balance they spend on verified offers.

→ links to coupon-wallet.html (→ vault.html)

_Label: The engine_

### AI Coupon

Creates offers inside limits a merchant sets, and places them across the exchange. Powers Zero Fee.

→ links to ai-coupon.html

### NFC Coupon

Tap points in malls, lobbies and offices, hosted by partners. Any merchant in the pool flows through them.
