## Not a merchant product. A network node.

The old model tied one tag to one merchant. The network ties every tap point to the whole pool, so a merchant leaving changes nothing for the node.

**Infographic — Old way vs now — one tag per merchant vs a shared pool feeding many tap points**

_Panel: The old way_

### The old way

_Label: Retired_
_[Diagram: One tag tied to one merchant; when the merchant leaves the tag goes dead]_

**One tag, one merchant, one fee.** When the merchant churned, the tag went dead and the fee stopped. Value never exceeded that merchant's own foot traffic.

_Panel: How it works now_

### How it works now

_Label: Live_
_[Diagram: A shared supply pool of merchants feeding several tap points; merchants come and go while the nodes stay live]_

**Many merchants, many nodes, no merchant fee.** A partner hosts the tap point. Any merchant in the pool flows through it, routed by AI Coupon. One leaves, another has already joined, and every node stays live. Each new node makes every merchant more valuable — and the reverse.

## Two ways to join

_Label: Host_

### Malls, property, HR and offices

You provide the surface: entrances, directories, lift lobbies, pantries. Mezzofy provides the tags, the inventory and the redemption rails. You share in the revenue on every Pass that moves through your site, and your visitors or staff get a reason to come back.

_Label: Supply_

### Merchants near a node

List on the exchange and your offers appear at tap points around you automatically. Zero Fee Passes cost you nothing; Charge Passes follow the usual 10%. Off-peak targeting works especially well here — a lunch offer at an office lobby at 11:45.

> Nothing to install. Set your limits once.

> **For customers, one growing network of physical places to collect verified offers.** That is what makes a tap point worth hosting, and it is why the network is built with partners rather than sold to them.
