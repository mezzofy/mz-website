Every Pass is tokenised and consumed on validation. A duplicate does not work, so promotion abuse does not reach your customers.

Merchants are onboarded and verified before their inventory enters the pool. You are not distributing scraped offers.

#### Full redemption trail

Every issuance, draw and redemption is recorded and reconcilable, which is what your finance and audit teams will ask for.

## Questions buyers ask

**Q: How long does integration take?**  
A: Pass and Vault direct needs no build and can be live in days. API integration is typically two to four weeks including certification, with a sandbox from day one.

**Q: Who handles a customer whose Pass will not validate?**  
A: Mezzofy does. Every Pass carries a guarantee, and a failed redemption is credited back automatically. Your support team is not in the loop unless you want to be.

**Q: Can I get inventory nobody else has?**  
A: Yes. Exclusive allocations and co-branded Passes are available against volume commitments. Ask during scoping.

**Q: What reporting do I receive?**  
A: Draw, distribution and redemption per Pass, per merchant and per campaign, reconcilable to settlement. Available in the portal and by API.

**Q: What happens to full-purchase Passes that expire unused?**  
A: On redemption base they can be swapped. On full purchase, expiry terms are set in your agreement. Ask about rollover credit before you choose full purchase.
