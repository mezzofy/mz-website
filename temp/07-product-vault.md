## Two things, kept separate on purpose

Balance goes down. The Vault fills up. Money only ever moves one way — unless a Pass fails to validate, in which case it comes straight back.

> **Why not one wallet?** A vault is a place things stay. A balance is something you spend down. Keeping them apart keeps the mental model honest for the customer, and keeps the stored-value component clearly identified for the people who regulate it.

## How a claim works

Flow:
- **Find** — Marketplace, tap point, or AI assistant
- **Claim** — A few cents from balance
- **Hold** — Lands in the Vault, ready
- **Present** — Validated once at the counter
- **Or credit back** — If it will not validate, balance is restored

Vault is available to enterprise buyers as a white-label component, so their customers hold Passes without ever seeing Mezzofy. [For buyers](for-distributors.html (→ for-buyers.html))
