# Mezzofy B2B Exchange Portal — Enterprise Buyer Terms

**Version:** 1.0 · **Effective Date:** 1 October 2026 · **Portal:** https://exchange.mezzofy.com

These Enterprise Buyer Terms (the "Agreement") govern how an Enterprise Buyer shops for and orders Merchant Passes on the Mezzofy B2B Exchange Portal. Every order must meet the Minimum Order Quantity, and every invoice must be paid in full before Passes are delivered. Buyers choose between a Pooled Model, where Mezzofy releases Passes from a shared pool as they are needed, and a Full Purchase Model, where 100% of the ordered quantity is delivered at once.

## 2. Definitions

- **"Exchange Portal"** means the Mezzofy B2B Exchange Portal at https://exchange.mezzofy.com, its APIs and related tools.
- **"Enterprise Buyer"** or **"Buyer"** means a business, such as a bank, telco, employer, HR or benefits platform, loyalty operator or corporate gifting company, that buys Passes for distribution to its own users, customers or employees.
- **"End User"** means an individual to whom the Buyer distributes a Pass.
- **"Merchant"** means the business that issues a Pass on Mezzofy's platform and provides the product or service it represents.
- **"Pass"** means a digital coupon, voucher, gift card, e-ticket or other redeemable entitlement issued by a Merchant, identified by a unique Token.
- **"Pass Terms"** means the conditions of a Pass set by its Merchant, including benefit, participating locations, validity, expiry and transferability.
- **"Listing"** means a Pass offered on the Exchange Portal, with its unit price, Minimum Order Quantity and available quantity.
- **"Order"** means a Buyer's request to buy a quantity of Passes from one or more Listings, once confirmed by Mezzofy.
- **"Minimum Order Quantity"** or **"MOQ"** means the minimum number of Passes, or minimum order value, that must be ordered for a Listing or an Order, as shown on the Exchange Portal or Order Form.
- **"Ordered Quantity"** means the total number of Passes in a paid Order.
- **"Pooled Model"** and **"Full Purchase Model"** have the meanings in clause 6.
- **"Balance Quantity"** means, under the Pooled Model, the part of the Ordered Quantity not yet released to the Buyer.
- **"Turnaround Time"** means the time Mezzofy needs to release Balance Quantity after a valid request, as set out in clause 6.5.
- **"Redemption"** means the validation of a Pass's Token by the Merchant, after which the Pass is marked used.

## 4. Shopping and Ordering

4.1 The Exchange Portal lets the Buyer browse Listings of Passes issued by Merchants, compare unit prices, Pass Terms, locations and availability, and place Orders. Mezzofy acts as the seller of the Passes to the Buyer on behalf of the Merchants and operates the exchange.

4.2 **Pricing.** The unit price of every Pass is fixed by its Merchant. Mezzofy does not set, discount or change Merchant prices. Listings and available quantities may change until an Order is confirmed, and the price in the Order Confirmation applies to that Order.

4.3 **Minimum Order Quantity.** Each Order must meet the MOQ for every Listing in it, and any minimum total Order value shown on the Exchange Portal or Order Form. Orders below the MOQ cannot be submitted. Mezzofy may agree a different MOQ in a signed Order Form.

4.4 When placing an Order, the Buyer must select its fulfilment model for each Listing: the Pooled Model or the Full Purchase Model (clause 6). The unit price is fixed by the Merchant and is the same under both models.

4.5 An Order is binding only when Mezzofy sends an Order Confirmation. Mezzofy may decline an Order, in whole or in part, where the Listing is no longer available, the Merchant has withdrawn it, or a pricing or system error has occurred.

4.6 The Buyer may cancel an Order free of charge at any time before it pays the invoice. Once the invoice is paid, the Order cannot be cancelled except as set out in clause 7.

## 6. Fulfilment Models and Delivery

6.1 For each Listing in an Order, the Buyer chooses one of two fulfilment models:

| | Pooled Model (redemption-based) | Full Purchase Model |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quantity available after payment | At least 25% of the Ordered Quantity, drawn from a shared pool | 100% of the Ordered Quantity |
| Balance Quantity | Topped up by Mezzofy whenever the available quantity falls below 25%, subject to the Turnaround Time | None — all Passes delivered at once |
| Pricing | Fixed by the Merchant | Fixed by the Merchant |
| Best suited to | Programmes where only part of the issued Passes are expected to be redeemed | Fixed allocations, gifting and campaigns needing every Pass upfront |

6.2 **Pooled Model.** Passes are held in a shared pool managed by Mezzofy, so that inventory can be used efficiently based on expected redemption. After the invoice is paid, Mezzofy makes available to the Buyer at least 25% of the Ordered Quantity (the "Minimum Available Quantity"), or the whole remaining Balance Quantity if that is less. The Buyer remains entitled to the full Ordered Quantity it has paid for.

6.3 **Full Purchase Model.** After the invoice is paid, Mezzofy delivers 100% of the Ordered Quantity, and Mezzofy holds no Balance Quantity for that Order.

6.4 **Pool management and top-ups.** Mezzofy monitors each Pooled Order. Whenever the Buyer's available, undistributed Passes fall below the Minimum Available Quantity (the "Low Threshold"), Mezzofy will automatically top them up from the Balance Quantity back to at least the Minimum Available Quantity. The Buyer may also request additional Balance Quantity through the Exchange Portal or API at any time before the Order's balance expiry date.

6.5 **Turnaround Time.** Mezzofy will complete each top-up within 7 business days of the Low Threshold being reached, and each additional Balance request within 7 business days of a valid request, or within the time stated in the Order Form or Support Policy. The Buyer acknowledges that Balance Quantity is not instantly available and must plan its requests to allow for the Turnaround Time. Turnaround Time is a target; a delay does not entitle the Buyer to a refund, but Mezzofy will keep the Buyer informed and fulfil the request as soon as possible.

6.6 **Balance expiry.** Balance Quantity must be requested before the earlier of the expiry of the Listing's Pass validity and defined months from the Order date. Unrequested Balance Quantity at that date will be: (a) forfeited; (b) converted to credit for future Orders; or (c) delivered automatically — to be decided.

6.7 If a Merchant cannot supply enough Passes to fulfil a Balance request, Mezzofy will offer Passes of equivalent value from comparable Merchants, subject to the Buyer's approval.

6.8 **Delivery.** Passes are delivered through the Exchange Portal as downloadable codes or links, by API into the Buyer's own app or rewards platform, or by direct issue to End Users' Marketplace accounts, as selected by the Buyer. Delivery is complete when the Passes are made available to the Buyer in the chosen channel.

6.9 Once delivered, the Buyer is responsible for keeping Passes and codes secure. Mezzofy is not responsible for Passes lost, leaked or misused after delivery, but will help void and reissue unredeemed Passes where it can verify they have not been used.

7.1 **Distribution to End Users.** The Buyer may distribute Passes to its End Users free of charge, as rewards, benefits or incentives, or at a price it sets, under its own branding. The Buyer must not resell Passes to other businesses, or list them on third-party marketplaces, without Mezzofy's written approval.

7.2 The Buyer must present each Pass's Pass Terms to End Users accurately and must not change the benefit, validity or conditions of a Pass. The Buyer is responsible for its own terms with End Users, including any promotions it runs with the Passes.

7.3 The Buyer must comply with all laws that apply to its distribution of Passes, including consumer protection, advertising, promotions, tax and data protection laws, and any rules that apply to it as a regulated financial institution or telco.

7.4 **Redemption.** End Users redeem Passes directly with the Merchant at the locations in the Pass Terms. Merchants are bound by their agreement with Mezzofy to honour every valid, unexpired, active Pass. Mezzofy's validation record is the reference for whether a Pass is active, redeemed, expired or voided.

7.5 **Merchant disputes.** The Merchant is responsible for the product or service behind each Pass. End User complaints about quality or service should be directed to the Merchant; Mezzofy will help the Buyer escalate unresolved issues.

7.6 **Merchant closure protection.** If a Merchant permanently closes or stops operating so that delivered, unredeemed Passes can no longer be redeemed, Mezzofy will, at the Buyer's request made within 90 days of notice of the closure, replace those Passes, and any affected Balance Quantity, with Passes of equivalent value from other Merchants on the Exchange Portal.

7.7 **No refunds.** Paid Orders are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled. Passes that expire unredeemed, whether with the Buyer or with End Users, are not refunded or replaced. The Buyer's remedies are the delivery of the Ordered Quantity, the replacement of Passes under clauses 6.7, 6.9 and 7.6, and the correction of any error by Mezzofy.

7.8 **Reporting.** Mezzofy will provide the Buyer with dashboards and reports in the Exchange Portal showing, for each Order, the Ordered Quantity, delivered and Balance Quantities, and Redemptions.
